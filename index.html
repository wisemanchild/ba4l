How did Reconstruction change political power for Black Americans?
Quick answer: Reconstruction expanded citizenship, voting rights, education, and political participation after the Civil War. Black men served in local, state, and federal office, but organized violence and later legal restrictions sharply reduced those gains.

How answers should work

Clear first. Evidence next. Context always.

The research assistant is designed to avoid vague answers and unsupported claims. Every serious answer should separate verified facts from interpretation and show the reader where the information came from.

✓Quick answer: a plain-language explanation without unnecessary jargon.
✓Historical background: the people, dates, laws, and events that shaped the issue.
✓Current relevance: how the subject connects to present conditions.
✓Research support: books, authors, court opinions, government records, and credible reporting.

Legal context

What law supports the current condition?

Answers should identify the law or court case, explain its effect, and distinguish federal rules from state-level changes.

Factual confidence

What is known, disputed, or uncertain?

When experts disagree, the assistant should show the major viewpoints and the evidence behind each one.
